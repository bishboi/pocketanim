# Rajasthan

Rajasthan is the largest state of India by area. It covers about 342,239 km², roughly a tenth of the country. Its capital is Jaipur, and it borders Pakistan to the west.

The Aravalli Range runs across the state from south-west to north-east. Its highest peak, Guru Shikhar near Mount Abu, rises to 1,722 metres. West of the Aravallis lies the Thar Desert, a land of sand dunes and scrub.

Rajasthan is one of the driest parts of India. Jaisalmer in the far west gets less than 200 mm of rain in a year, while the south-east gets far more from the summer monsoon. Summer days in the desert often pass 45 °C.

The Chambal is the state's largest perennial river. It flows north-east through Kota to join the Yamuna. The Luni flows south-west into the Rann of Kutch and is salty for much of its length.

About 68 million people lived in Rajasthan at the 2011 census. Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota and Udaipur are its largest cities, and farming, mining and tourism are its main industries.
