Japan owes more than any other rich country: over two hundred percent of everything it makes in a year.
America owes about one hundred and twenty percent. Germany, about sixty.
So why hasn't Japan gone broke?
Its debt is a pile of IOUs: government bonds, promises to pay back later.
And almost all of them are held at home. Only about eight percent sit abroad.
The biggest holder is Japan's own central bank, which owns around half.
A country that owes mostly to itself can keep borrowing far longer than anyone expects.
